Movement I

The one number, formally

The plate’s whole argument descends from a single quantity: ε, the elasticity of the labour supply a single firm faces. Everything else — the wage, the markdown, the gendered wedge, the case for a floor — is a deterministic function of it. Here is exactly what the code computes.

MRP(L) = 2 − L
Marginal revenue product — the firm’s downward-sloping demand for labour.
wS(L) = L1/ε
Iso-elastic inverse supply, normalised through (1,1): the wage needed to attract L workers. Supply elasticity is exactly ε.
MCL(L) = wS(L)·(1 + 1/ε)
Marginal cost of labour. The wedge (1+1/ε) over the wage is the whole of monopsony.
Lm : MCL = MRP
The firm hires until marginal cost meets marginal product (solved by bisection in the code).
wm = wS(Lm)
It then pays only the wage that supply requires at that headcount — below MRP.
μ = 1/(ε+1)
The claimed markdown: the gap between product and wage, as a share of product.
share = ε/(ε+1)
The claimed wage share of product — Pigou’s rate of exploitation, complemented.
Why this is checkableThe code asserts two things at once: a numerical equilibrium (bisection on MCL = MRP) and a pair of closed forms (μ, share). They are only consistent if the solved wage obeys wm/MRP = ε/(ε+1). That is a falsifiable claim — so I falsified it, or tried to.

Movement III

The identity is deeper than the demand curve

Is μ = 1/(ε+1) an artifact of the particular choice MRP = 2 − L? No. The markdown ratio falls out of the optimality condition alone: at MCL = MRP, wm(1+1/ε) = MRP, so wm/MRP = ε/(ε+1) for any demand curve. To show it rather than assert it, I re-solved the equilibrium under four different MRP curves — including one not passing through (1,1) and one that is non-linear.

FindingAcross all four curves the ratio lands on ε/(ε+1) to five decimals. The markdown is a property of constant-elasticity supply, not of the demand the firm happens to face — exactly as the theory says, and exactly what the apparatus implicitly relies on.

Movement V

Honesty audit

Held against the discipline these instruments set for themselves — model one mechanism honestly, show rather than assert, label analogies, name sources — the plate is mostly exemplary, with two fair gaps.

Where it holds ✓

  • One mechanism, honestly. A single textbook monopsony, stated plainly in the coda: “the markdown is exactly one over the elasticity plus one.” No false precision.
  • Shows, doesn’t assert. The headline identity is derivable and derived; this audit confirms the code obeys it to 10−6.
  • Epistemically candid. The competitive wage is rendered as “a ridge in fog” — the one quantity the model “can never hand over.” The unobservable counterfactual is named as such.
  • Normalisation disclosed. The coda states the curve is normalised so competitive equilibrium sits at unit wage and unit employment.

Where it could answer for itself ·

  • No named authorities. The economics is Robinson (1933) and Manning’s Monopsony in Motion; Pigou named the exploitation rate. The plate cites none of them — 0 attributions in the visible text.
  • The ε=60 sentinel. The code treats ε ≥ 60 as “competitive,” snapping the markdown to 0%. A literal ε=60 would still mark wages down ~1.6%. A defensible simplification — worth a footnote it does not have.
  • No not-advice disclaimer. If this plate were ever pointed at a live wage-and-hour matter, it would need the usual “illustrative, not legal advice” guard. As a teaching plate it arguably does not — but the line should be explicit.
