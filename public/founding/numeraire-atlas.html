A Periodic Table
of Economic Life

Every ordinary price — the wage, the rent, the rate — is a verdict on a hidden contest, commonly mistaken for a fact of nature. These are the elements: twenty-six primitives of economic life, each the subject of a volume, each to be turned inside out with the rigor of the seminar and the grace of the essay. The work is to fix a numéraire of honesty against which the verdicts can finally be read. Volume I, The Wage, is complete; Volume II, The Rent, is under way.

Twenty-six primitives of economic life, grouped in six families, each to be turned inside out at the seam where the seminar and the essay usually part — with the rigor of the one and the grace of the other, and each scrupulous about the exact edge of what can be known. Volume I, The Wage, is complete in twelve plates; Volume II, The Rent, is under way. Set in Fraunces and Newsreader. The argument remains open.
