Earlier editions retain their own plans and reading sequences; the Margin edition’s Wage, Rent, Rate, Premium, Spread, Margin, Surplus, Utility, Stock, and Power now each have all twelve plates. A finished treatment in one edition does not fill a different edition’s planned slot.

  9. ixThe Honorcodes of honor and the professions; what binds the members of a trade — the guild, the bar, the bench — beyond the law that governs them.
  10. xThe Cascadepreference falsification and the tipping of norms; how a rule obeyed for generations, but privately rejected, can collapse almost overnight.
  11. xiThe Conspicuousconspicuous consumption and emulation; how status and the watching eye of others govern what we buy, and how much is bought to be seen buying it.
